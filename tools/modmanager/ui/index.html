HewDraw Remix

A fan-made gameplay overhaul of Smash Ultimate: movement and techniques from the older games come back, the whole cast gets new and reworked moves, and it brings its own competitive stage list.

HDR only works with every other mod turned off.

GitHub Releases & changelog Discord

What it changes

  • Movement and advanced techniques inspired by Melee, Brawl, Project M and Smash 4
  • New moves and reworked attacks for every fighter, with its own balance
  • Changes to physics, shields, ledges and landing
  • A stage list of its own, with remade stages and alternate versions (stage_alts, stage_config)

You have to turn every other mod off

  • HewDraw Remix only works with every other mod turned off. With your other mods on, matches crash.
  • Turning on the switch above Apply to game offers to turn them off for you. All on, in My mods, brings them back when you're done with HDR.
  • Then press Apply to game (and Build plugins into the game the first time).
  • Online, everyone in the room needs HDR on, the same version.

Credits

HewDraw Remix is made by the HewDraw Remix Dev Team (HDR-Development) and its contributors. HDR includes stages, UI and animations by many independent mod creators, used with their permission, and its own code is open source (AGPL-3.0).

WindowSmash doesn't ship HDR: the download comes straight from HDR's official releases on GitHub. Thanks to jugarrit for the fixes that let HDR run here. HDR isn't made by or connected with Nintendo.

Modpacks

Got a modpack from a friend?

Paste its code, or drop its .wsmodpack file on this window. The mods it needs are downloaded, checked for clashes and turned on together.

Add a mod

From a link

A GameBanana page, or a direct download link (Discord, Google Drive, Dropbox, GitHub releases...).

What works

  • Skins, including new costume slots (c08 and up)
  • Stages, UI, music, effects, text and param patches (.xmsbt, .prcx, .prcxml)
  • Movesets and new characters: their Skyline plugins (.nro) are compiled into the game (Build plugins into the game), on the real ARCropolis, Smashline 2, One Slot Effects and CSK Collection (Settings: Community plugins)
  • Any number of new characters: Apply to game makes the select screen smaller by itself to fit them all

Community plugins

The Skyline plugins movesets and new characters are built on, from their official releases: the same ones a Switch runs, compiled into the game with your mods' own (Build plugins into the game gets any that's missing).

Share WindowSmash Mods

Make an installer your friends can run on Windows or Mac to get this mod manager. (To share mods too, make it from a modpack.)

Uninstall WindowSmash Mods

Back to the game as it was: the mods are turned off in the game, the modded data is deleted and this mod manager is removed (files it replaced are put back). You choose whether your downloaded mods are deleted too.
